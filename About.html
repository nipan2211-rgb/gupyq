About Us

Student Academic Resource Archive is an independent educational initiative developed to provide organized access to academic resources for students, researchers, and educators associated with Gauhati University. The platform brings together previous year question papers, syllabi, admission resources, study materials, and other useful academic documents in one easily accessible location.

The primary objective of this archive is to simplify access to educational resources, support digital preservation, and reduce the time students spend searching for important academic information. By organizing these materials in a structured manner, the archive promotes efficient learning and encourages the responsible use of digital academic resources.

Project Development

This website has been independently designed and developed by Nipan Kalita, a student of the Department of Library and Information Science, Gauhati University, as part of an academic project focusing on the design and development of a web-based digital archive for educational resources.

This website was developed as part of an academic project carried out under the guidance of Dr. Badan Barman, Assistant Professor, Department of Library and Information Science, Gauhati University.

Vision

The long-term vision of Student Academic Resource Archive is to develop a comprehensive, student-friendly academic resource platform that supports learning, digital preservation, and easier access to educational materials. The archive aims to encourage knowledge sharing, improve academic preparedness, and contribute to a stronger digital learning environment for students.
