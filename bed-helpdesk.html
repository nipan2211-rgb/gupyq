Basic details of Gauhati University B.Ed Common Entrance Test

Gauhati University conducts the B.Ed. Common Entrance Test (GUBEDCET) every year for admission into the two-year Bachelor of Education programme. The exam schedule for GUBEDCET 2026 has been announced. Online applications will be available from Coming to 22 June 2026, and the entrance test will be held on 19 July 2026. Admission to affiliated B.Ed colleges is done through GUBEDCET merit list and seat allocation.

Rules, Fee and Centres

Marking Scheme

+4 / -1

Four marks for each correct answer and one mark deducted for each wrong answer.

Cut-off Marks

80 / 60

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GUBEDCET? +
GUBEDCET is the Gauhati University B.Ed Common Entrance Test for admission into B.Ed colleges affiliated under Gauhati University.
When will GU B.Ed application start? +
The online application will start from Coming .
What is the last date to apply? +
The last date to apply is 22 June 2026.
When will the exam be held? +
The GUBEDCET 2026 entrance test will be held on 19 July 2026.
Is there negative marking? +
Yes. One mark will be deducted for each wrong answer.
