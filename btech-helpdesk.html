Admission Process

The admission process for the B.Tech and B.Tech (Lateral) programmes offered by Gauhati University Institute of Science and Technology for the academic session 2026–27 is in process.

B.Tech Admission Modes

  1. B.Tech in ECE and R&AI

    A maximum of 20% of the total seats will be filled by candidates qualified through JEE (Main) 2026.

    Counselling for admission based on JEE (Main) 2026 rank will be conducted at GUIST. The counselling schedule will be published on the Gauhati University website.

    The remaining 80% of the seats will be filled through CEE (Assam) 2026 counselling conducted by GUIST according to the notified schedule.

  2. B.Tech in Biotechnology

    50% of the seats will be filled through an entrance examination conducted by the concerned department.

    From the remaining seats, 20% will be filled through JEE (Main) 2026 and 80% through CEE (Assam) 2026 qualified candidates.

  3. Permanent Residence Certificate

    Only candidates possessing a valid Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC) of Assam are eligible to apply for admission.

Online Application

The online application form will be available through the Gauhati University SAMARTH admission portal.

The last date for submitting the application will be mentioned in the application form. The list of candidates eligible to participate in counselling will be published on the Gauhati University website and the SAMARTH portal after the last date of application.

B.Tech (Lateral) Admission – 2026

Admission to the B.Tech (Lateral) programme will be conducted through counselling based on JLEE 2026 rank.

The admission criteria and relaxation of marks in the qualifying examination will follow the JLEE (Assam) 2026 norms and the AICTE Approval Process Handbook 2024–2027.

Other Reserved Seats for North-Eastern States

Seven seats in the B.Tech programme are reserved for candidates from other North-Eastern states. One seat is reserved for each eligible state other than Assam.

Candidates seeking admission under this category must contact the respective Directorate of Higher Education or Directorate of Technical Education of their state.

Documents Required at the Time of Admission

B.Tech and B.Tech (Lateral Entry) Admission 2026

Candidates must submit self-attested copies of the following documents at the time of admission.

  • Admit Card or Score Card of CEE (Assam) 2026 or JEE (Main) 2026
  • Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC) of Assam
  • HSLC or Class 10 Pass Certificate/Admit Card as proof of age
  • Higher Secondary (10+2) Pass Certificate
  • HSLC or Class 10 Mark Sheet/Grade Sheet
  • Higher Secondary (10+2) Mark Sheet/Grade Sheet
  • Valid SC, ST, OBC or other caste certificate issued by the appropriate authority and approved by the Government of Assam
  • OBC candidates must submit a valid Non-Creamy Layer Certificate. The OBC NCL certificate is compulsory according to Gauhati University rules.
  • Diploma examination mark sheet, grade sheet and certificate for candidates applying for B.Tech Lateral Entry
  • Appropriate supporting documents for candidates applying under other reserved categories
  • Gap Certificate, wherever applicable
  • UGC Anti-Ragging Undertaking Form
    Every student must submit the online Anti-Ragging Undertaking before admission.
