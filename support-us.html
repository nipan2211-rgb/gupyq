Support Us

The Gauhati University Digital Archive is a student-driven educational initiative developed to provide free and organized access to academic resources such as previous year question papers, syllabi, study materials, and research content.

Maintaining this website requires annual expenses for domain registration, hosting, storage, and technical maintenance. To continue providing these services freely for students and researchers, your financial support will be highly appreciated.

Even a small contribution can help support the growth, maintenance, and future development of this digital archive platform.

Support the Archive Beyond Donations: Students, alumni, and faculty members can also contribute to the archive by sharing scanned previous year question papers, syllabi, study materials, and other academic resources. Every contribution helps us build a more comprehensive and organized repository for the Gauhati University community.
